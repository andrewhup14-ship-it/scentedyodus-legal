Privacy Policy — Herald Content Scheduler

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Herald Content Scheduler ("Herald") is a personal-use content scheduling tool. It generates and schedules social media content (Pinterest pins, TikTok posts) for a single small online shop. Herald is not a consumer-facing product and does not collect data from members of the public.

What data Herald accesses

Herald connects to Pinterest and TikTok developer APIs solely to publish content on behalf of the shop owner's own connected accounts. It accesses:

  • Account authentication tokens (OAuth), used only to publish posts to the owner's own accounts
  • Basic account/profile information required by the platform APIs to confirm the connected account
  • Post performance metrics (views, clicks, engagement) for the owner's own published posts

What Herald does not do

  • Herald does not collect, store, or process personal data belonging to third parties or the public
  • Herald does not sell, share, or transfer any data to third parties
  • Herald does not use collected data for advertising or profiling

Data storage

All data is stored on a private server controlled by the shop owner and is not accessible to the public. Data is retained only as long as needed to operate the scheduling tool and is deleted upon request.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the shop owner via the shop's Etsy storefront messaging.

This policy applies only to the Herald Content Scheduler application and its use of Pinterest and TikTok developer APIs.
